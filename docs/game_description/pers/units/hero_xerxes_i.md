## Guide

Xerxes I (Xšayāršā) is the Persian infantry archer hero — a 1000 HP
archer (27 pierce from 60 m) who walks at infantry pace and costs a
modest 200 food + 200 wood + 150 metal. He is a support hero with **two**
auras: "Administrator" (100 m radius: workers +25% build rate and +15%
gather speed) and "Invader of Greece" (60 m radius: siege, elephants and
champion infantry +25% health). Park him in a woodline or a forward base
during the economy phases to accelerate gathering and construction, then
march him with the late-game assault force — rams, war elephants and
Immortals all get the health boost — where his own bow and 1000 HP make
him a passable back-line fighter. 0 population, 1 hero alive at a time,
trained at the Winter Palace (City phase).
