## Guide

Darius I the Great (Dārayavahuš) is the Persian chariot archer hero: a
1500 HP, 6/8/25-armored chariot shooting 28 pierce arrows from 60 m — the
tankiest of the three Persian heroes, but also the most expensive (360
food + 250 wood + 300 metal, 60 s). His "Leadership" aura is **global**
(no radius): while Darius lives, every own soldier, siege engine, land
trader and merchant ship moves +15% faster — a whole-empire logistic and
strategic speedup that no other Persian hero matches. Since the aura is
map-wide, he is best kept alive behind the lines (garrisoned or with a
bodyguard) rather than risked in the fight. 0 population, 1 hero alive at
a time, trained at the Winter Palace (City phase).
