## Basic stats

- **Name:** Will to Fight
- **Cost:** 1500 food, 1500 wood, 1500 stone, 1500 metal
- **Research time:** 40 s
- **Requirements:** `{"all": [{"tech": "phase_city"},{"notciv": "han"}]}` — Unlocked in City Phase.
- **Effect:** Soldiers, Siege Engines, and Ships +25% attack damage.
- **Modifications:**
  - ×1.25 Attack/Melee/Damage/Hack
  - ×1.25 Attack/Melee/Damage/Pierce
  - ×1.25 Attack/Melee/Damage/Crush
  - ×1.25 Attack/Ranged/Damage/Hack
