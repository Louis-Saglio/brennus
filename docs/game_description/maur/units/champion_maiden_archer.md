## Guide

The Maiden Guard Archer (Visha Kanya) is the Mauryan ranged champion — a
very fast skirmish archer: **walk 12.3 m/s** with a 15.5-pierce bow at
45 m (0.3 s prepare). She is fragile (90 HP, 1 hack armor) but cheap (100
wood + 90 metal, 15 s) and trained at the palace (City phase). Paired
with the melee Maiden Guard she gives the Mauryans a fast, mobile
champion duo that outruns and outkites most infantry; keep her behind
the swordswomen, since anything that catches her kills her.
