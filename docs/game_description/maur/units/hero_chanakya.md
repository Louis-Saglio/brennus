## Guide

Acharya Chanakya is the Mauryan healer hero — a 1000 HP, 4/8/4-armored
support hero with **no attack**, who instead heals Humans (15 HP every 2
s, i.e. 7.5 HP/s, within 20 m). His two auras are the economic engine of
the civ: "Teacher" is **global** — every technology costs −20% resources
and −30% research time — and "Regeneration" gives Humans within 35 m
+0.8 HP/s. With Chanakya alive, the Mauryan tech tree (temple techs,
blacksmith, farming) is the cheapest in the game, and he doubles as a
field healer for the army. Keep him with the front or the research
campus, never in melee. 0 population, 1 hero alive at a time, trained at
the palace (City phase).
