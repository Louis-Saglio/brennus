## Guide

Hannibal Barca is the Carthaginian elephant hero — one of the game's few
elephant-mounted heroes (shared with the Kushite, Mauryan, Ptolemaic and
Seleucid heroes). At 1500 HP with 10/10/25 armor he is among the tankiest
heroes available, and his trunk attack (60 hack + 90 crush every 1.5 s at
5 m range) crushes structures as well as units, so he doubles as a
slow-moving siege engine against enemy buildings. He is metal-heavy (400 metal) and slow (walk 9 m/s, the infantry pace), so he is a deliberate late-game investment rather than a raider. His "Tactician" aura is the real payoff: every own or allied Soldier and Siege engine within 60 m gets +1 capture attack strength and +20% melee and ranged damage, so keep him with the main army when pushing, capturing or defending. Like all heroes he costs 0 population and is limited to 1 alive at a time, trained at the fortress (city phase).
