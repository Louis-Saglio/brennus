# units/heroes/athen_hero_iphicrates_1

Athenian-specific aura of 0 A.D. 0.28.0 — only the athenians can have it. See `docs/game_description/athen/auras/README.md` for the method; shared auras are documented in `docs/game_description/generic/auras/`.

Data file: `simulation/data/auras/units/heroes/athen_hero_iphicrates_1.json`.

## Basic stats

- **Name:** Formation Reforms
- **Type:** range
- **Radius:** 40 m
- **Affects:** Soldier
- **Description:** Soldiers +2 crush, hack, pierce resistance.
- **Modifications:**
  - +2 Resistance/Entity/Damage/Hack
  - +2 Resistance/Entity/Damage/Pierce
  - +2 Resistance/Entity/Damage/Crush

## Athenian

- attached by `units/athen/hero_iphicrates`

Note: Iphicrates' army aura — +2 to all three armor types for every own
soldier within 40 m, the largest flat armor aura among the civs
documented so far (Caros and Chandragupta give +1). On the already-
armored hoplites it makes the Athenian line grind through enemy fire;
keep Iphicrates in the middle of the phalanx.
