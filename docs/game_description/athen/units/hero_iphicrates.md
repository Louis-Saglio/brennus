## Guide

Iphicrates is the Athenian javelin hero — 1000 HP throwing an enormous
**62-pierce javelin** at 30 m (1.5 s repeat), the hardest-hitting ranged
hero among the civs documented so far. His auras are army-scale:
"Formation Reforms" (soldiers within 40 m +2 hack, pierce and crush
armor — a big defensive buff) and "Peltast Reforms" (**global**:
infantry javelineers +15% walk speed). With him alive, the Athenian
javelineer line is faster, and anything fighting near him is markedly
tougher. Trained at the prytaneion (City phase). 0 population, 1 hero
alive at a time.
