# units/heroes/cart_hero_maharbal

Carthaginian-specific aura of 0 A.D. 0.28.0 — only the carthaginians can have it. See `docs/game_description/cart/auras/README.md` for the method; shared auras are documented in `docs/game_description/generic/auras/`.

Data file: `simulation/data/auras/units/heroes/cart_hero_maharbal.json`.

## Basic stats

- **Name:** Cavalry Commander
- **Type:** range
- **Radius:** 60 m
- **Affects:** Melee Cavalry
- **Description:** Melee Cavalry +30% melee attack damage.
- **Modifications:**
  - ×1.3 Attack/Melee/Damage/Hack
  - ×1.3 Attack/Melee/Damage/Pierce
  - ×1.3 Attack/Melee/Damage/Crush

## Carthaginian

- attached by `units/cart/hero_maharbal`

Note: the largest damage aura in the Carthaginian kit (+30%), but limited to Melee Cavalry — the Italic Cavalry, the Gallic/Iberian mercenary cavalry and the Sacred Band Cavalry. It does not buff the Numidian cavalry javelineers (ranged) or infantry. Keep Maharbal in the middle of the cavalry wing to get the most out of it.
