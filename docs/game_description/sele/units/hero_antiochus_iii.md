## Guide

Antiochus III the Great (Antiokhos G' Megas) is the Seleucid cavalry
hero — a 1200 HP spear cavalryman (16 hack + 12 pierce, 1.75× vs
cavalry) fast enough (run 25.2 m/s) to lead charges and run down
raiders. His aura, "Ilarchès" (+2 hack/pierce/crush armor for all own
cavalry within 45 m), is a blanket durability buff for the civ's
cavalry-heavy roster — horse archers, javelineers, companion cavalry,
cataphracts and chariots. Pick him when the plan is cavalry screens and
raids: the armor bonus compounds on the already-armored cataphract line.
0 population, City phase, trained at the **civic centre**, subject to
the global limit of 1 hero alive at a time (which also gates the second
civic centre — see `civ.md`).
