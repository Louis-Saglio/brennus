# units/heroes/sele_hero_antiochus_iv

Seleucid-specific aura of 0 A.D. 0.28.0 — only the seleucids can have it. See `docs/game_description/sele/auras/README.md` for the method; shared auras are documented in `docs/game_description/generic/auras/`.

Data file: `simulation/data/auras/units/heroes/sele_hero_antiochus_iv.json`.

## Basic stats

- **Name:** Renowned Conqueror
- **Type:** range
- **Radius:** 80 m
- **Affects:** Structure, Ship, Siege
- **Affected players:** Enemy
- **Description:** Enemy Structures, Ships, and Siege Engines −20% health.
- **Modifications:**
  - ×0.8 Health/Max

## Seleucids

- attached by `units/sele/hero_antiochus_iv`

Note: a siege-support debuff — every enemy building, ship and siege
engine within 80 m of Antiochus IV fights at 80% health. It makes him
the hero to park with the assault force: enemy towers and fortresses
lose a fifth of their HP before the first blow lands, and wall-breaking
and naval fights get that much shorter. It does nothing to enemy units,
so bring him to structure fights, not open-field brawls. He is a 1200 HP
cavalry swordsman (26 hack), sturdy enough to stand in the danger zone
where the aura is useful.
