- **Radius:** 60 m
- **Affects:** Soldier, Siege
- **Description:** Soldiers and Siege Engines −20% melee and ranged attack repeat time.
- **Modifications:**
  - ×0.8 Attack/Melee/RepeatTime
  - ×0.8 Attack/Ranged/RepeatTime

## Ptolemaic

- attached by `units/ptol/hero_cleopatra_vii`

Note: Cleopatra's offensive aura — a −20% attack repeat time is a +25%
damage output for every own soldier and siege engine within 60 m,
stacking with whatever other damage auras are present. The strongest of
the three Ptolemaic combat auras; keep Cleopatra with the main army, not
in the back.
