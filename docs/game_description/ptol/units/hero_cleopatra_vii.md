## Guide

Cleopatra VII Philopator (Kleopatra H' Philopatōr) is the Ptolemaic
infantry archer hero — a 1000 HP archer (27 pierce from 60 m) with
**three auras**. "Patriot" (soldiers and siege within 60 m: −20% melee and
ranged attack repeat time) is a raw +25% damage output for any army she
marches with — the strongest offensive aura of the three heroes. The two
"Consort" auras are hero-targeted: allied heroes within 30 m get +10%
health, enemy heroes within 30 m get −10% — a niche effect, but useful
in hero duels and for buffing your own hero pick. 0 population, 1 hero
alive at a time, trained at the Temple of Isis (City phase).
