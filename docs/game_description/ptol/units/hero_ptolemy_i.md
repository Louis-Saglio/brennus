## Guide

Ptolemy I Soter (Ptolemaios A' Sōter) is the Ptolemaic elephant hero —
1500 HP with 10/10/25 armor and the standard elephant trunk attack (60
hack + 90 crush every 1.5 s at 5 m), doubling as a slow-moving siege
engine. He carries **two auras**: "Patron of Construction" (builders
within 60 m +10% build rate) and "Mercenary Patron" (global: every own
mercenary −35% resource costs — metal for the infantry mercs, metal+food
for the cavalry mercs). The second aura is the real economic engine of
the Ptolemaic mid-game: with Ptolemy I alive, a mercenary spearman drops
from 60 to 39 metal. Keep him near the expansion front to build civic
centres and colonies faster while your mercenary camp churns out cheap
hires. 0 population, 1 hero alive at a time, trained at the Temple of
Isis (City phase).
