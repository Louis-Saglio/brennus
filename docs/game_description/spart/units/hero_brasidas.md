## Guide

Brasidas is the Spartan swordsman hero — 1000 HP, 12/12/25 armor and a
26-hack sword (0.75 s repeat). His aura, "Helot Reforms" (+25% ranged
pierce damage and +1 to all armor for own citizen infantry javelineers
within 60 m), is the skirmisher-line amplifier: it turns the Helot
Skirmisher — the civ's only citizen ranged infantry — into a
significantly sturdier and harder-hitting screen, and its wide 60 m
radius covers a whole skirmish line. Pick Brasidas when the plan leans
on javelineers (and to offset the Helot Economy damage penalty); he
fights at the front where the aura matters. 0 population, City phase,
trained at the **gerousia**, free with the team bonus, subject to the
global limit of 1 hero alive at a time.
